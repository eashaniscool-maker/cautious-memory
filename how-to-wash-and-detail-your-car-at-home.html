How to Wash and Detail Your Car at Home

Wash in shade with a clean microfibre mitt and the two-bucket method: one bucket for soapy water and one for rinsing the mitt. This keeps dirt away from the paint.

Rinse the car first to remove loose grit, then wash from top to bottom. Dry with a soft microfibre towel to avoid water spots, and clean the wheels last with a separate brush.

Finish with wax or a paint sealant every few months. It protects against sun and rain and makes later washes faster. Vacuum the interior and wipe the dashboard with a gentle cleaner.
