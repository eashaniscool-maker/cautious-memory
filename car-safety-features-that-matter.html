Car Safety Features That Actually Matter

Start with the basics: airbags, ABS and electronic stability control. Stability control in particular helps prevent skids and has been proven to reduce serious crashes.

Automatic emergency braking can stop or slow the car when you fail to react, and is one of the most useful modern features. Lane keeping and blind spot monitoring add value on highways.

Check independent crash test ratings such as Global NCAP or Euro NCAP before buying. A high star rating reflects how the whole car protects its occupants, not just the feature list.
