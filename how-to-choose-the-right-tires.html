How to Choose the Right Tires for Your Car

Tires are the only part of your car touching the road. Read the sidewall: a code like 205/55 R16 gives the width in millimetres, the height as a percentage of width and the rim size in inches.

Match the tire to how you drive. Touring and all-season tires suit daily commuting, performance tires add grip in dry conditions, and all-terrain tires help on rough roads at the cost of some noise.

Replace tires when tread reaches 1.6 mm, or sooner if you see cracks or bulges. Check the four-digit date code too, since rubber hardens with age even when tread looks fine.
