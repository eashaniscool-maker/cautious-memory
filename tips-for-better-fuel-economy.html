10 Tips for Better Fuel Economy

Smooth driving saves the most fuel. Accelerate gently, look far ahead and let the car coast instead of braking late. Hard acceleration and sudden stops can waste a noticeable share of fuel.

Keep tires properly inflated, remove unused roof racks and clear heavy items from the boot. Extra weight and drag both raise consumption, especially at highway speeds.

Service the car on schedule, since a clogged air filter or worn spark plugs hurt efficiency. Use air conditioning sensibly and combine short trips into one longer journey.
