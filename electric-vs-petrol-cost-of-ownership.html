Electric vs Petrol: Real Cost of Ownership

Purchase price is only the beginning. An electric car usually costs more upfront, but charging at home is far cheaper per kilometre than petrol, and the gap grows the more you drive.

Servicing favours electric cars too. There is no oil to change and brake pads last longer thanks to regenerative braking. Tires can wear faster because of the extra weight and instant torque.

Depreciation and charging access decide the final result. If you can charge at home or work, electric often wins over five years. If you rely on public chargers or drive little, a petrol car can still be the cheaper choice.
